With the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, millions of people crossed into neighbouring countries. A large number chose Moldova as their primary destination.

    372Kborder entries in March 2022 alone

    Not everyone chose to stay in Moldova. The larger part moved on to România or elsewhere, many by air.

    87%of March 2022 departures went to countries other than Ukraine

    Even as new refugees kept arriving from Ukraine, movements grew more complex: many people travel back and forth to check on their homes and families. At the same time, Ukrainians who had settled in other European countries have relocated to Moldova.

    In March 2023 the Government of Moldova introduced Temporary Protection (TP), taking over from the emergency regulations. The number of people who have obtained it grew steadily.

    88Kpeople cumulatively obtained TP, 1 March 2023 – , per the Inspectorate General for Migration

    In February 2026 the Government launched the extension of Temporary Protection, which also served as a validation of who remains in the country. The exercise closed at the end of April 2026.
